## 7. THE HONEST BOTTOM LINE

**Rewritten 2026-08-03 after the campaign. The previous version of this section
claimed "the theory is untouched and still stands"; W3 makes that false.**

The mathematics is intact: no result contradicted Lemma 1, Lemma 2, Theorem 1 or
Theorem 2. But one of the theory's predictions, the rate exponent, does not
appear on real adapters, and the regime where it would be visible is not reached
because everything is flat from b=2 up.

The method survives its hardest control and is stronger than it looked on 08-02:
**rd-encoder ridge beats a coefficient-tuned Task Arithmetic on three of four
bases by 32-39% and ties on the fourth**, beats a properly implemented KnOTS on
four of four, and beats TIES on four of four. A1 additionally vindicates the
floor-zero regime on a correctly initialised cohort and dissolves reviewer W2.

What is genuinely lost is the reason a reader should care. The June reframe made
the operational audit the centerpiece on external advice that the theory was too
weak to headline. W5 removes its support, and the theory-first framing was
already judged insufficient. Both available centerpieces are now compromised at
once, roughly a month from the ICLR deadline. That, not any single finding, is
the problem.

What survives as a field-level claim is **W1 alone**: across four bases the
conventional `1/T` merge coefficient is undertuned, TA's optimum is 0.50 to 0.75,
and tuning that one scalar closes most of the gap published methods claim over
it. The scorer bugs are **our** bugs in `downstream_metrics.py` and are a
disclosure, not a contribution. Do not write them up as a finding about the
field.

The realistic paths are a scaled correlation study, a cross-cohort ranking result
if the A1 matrix supports one, or TMLR without the deadline. Decide after step 0.
