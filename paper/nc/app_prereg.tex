Every empirical claim in this paper is governed by a registration document
committed to version control before the compute it governs was run. Each
document states the predictions, the decision rule, and the consequence text
for every possible outcome, including the outcomes that would cost us a claim.
Thresholds were never moved after data were seen, and analyzers refuse to
report until every cell they need exists.

The documents are in the directory \texttt{paper/prereg/} of the public
repository, at \url{\repourl}. Table~\ref{tab:commits} gives, for each registration this
paper relies on, the commit that fixed its rules, the commit that added the
generator or implementation, and the commit that added the result. A reader
can check any row with
\begin{center}
\texttt{git merge-base -{}-is-ancestor <rules> <result>}
\end{center}
which exits zero exactly when the rules commit precedes the result. The hashes
are those of the public repository, whose history is filtered to remove
private correspondence and large build artifacts; filtering rewrites hashes
but not the order of commits, which is what the check depends on. Hashes cited
inside the registration documents themselves are those of our working
repository at the time and do not resolve publicly; we leave them unedited,
since an unedited document is the point.
