This appendix collects the evaluation details, the falsification tests
for the (Llama-3.1, GSM8K \textsc{em}) inversion, and the per-seed
stability analysis behind \S\ref{subsec:exp-downstream-gsm8k}.

\paragraph{Evaluation details.} GSM8K: each merged checkpoint answers
the first $n = 500$ test prompts with greedy decoding (max $256$ new
tokens, $0$-shot CoT prompt matching the per-task training format);
the final numeric answer is extracted by a four-pattern regex
(\verb|#### N|, ``answer is $N$'', trailing equation, or last number)
that succeeded in $95\%$ of generations in an $n = 100$ pilot; EM is
exact match to the gold value. HumanEval: pass@1 over $n = 164$
problems, greedy CoT, subprocess sandbox with $5$s timeout per test.

\paragraph{H1, chat-template emission: falsified.} The Llama-3
family's chat template introduces special tokens
(\verb!<|begin_of_text|>!, \verb!<|start_header_id|>!,
\verb!<|end_header_id|>!, \verb!<|eot_id|>!) that a merged model might
emit unpredictably under aggressive trim/quantize compression. A
controlled probe (each method generates $n = 100$ GSM8K answers with
\verb|skip_special_tokens=False|) shows all five methods emit exactly
$1$ special token per generation on average (TA $1.00$, TIES $0.98$,
DARE $1.00$, KnOTS $1.00$, TVQ$_2$ $0.99$), and $\geq 98\%$ of
generations emit exactly one (the trailing \verb!<|eot_id|>!). No
method-specific emission exists.

\paragraph{H2, adapter $\Delta$ magnitude distribution: falsified.}
For each (model, task) adapter the authors materialized
$\Delta_t = \mathrm{scaling}\cdot B_t A_t$ per attention projection and
measured five shape statistics on $|\Delta_t|$
($p_{99}/\mathrm{median}$, excess kurtosis, top-$20\%$ threshold over
median, fraction within $\pm 10\%$ of that threshold, coefficient of
variation). Llama-3.1 sits squarely mid-pack on every statistic
(kurtosis $4.98$ vs.\ Mistral $3.94$, Qwen $4.64$, Yi $5.26$); its
distribution is neither flatter nor more peaked than the others.

\paragraph{H3, cross-metric sign-election contamination:
falsified.} If TIES's sign election were biased on Llama-3.1 in a way
that hurts deployable accuracy, the inversion should reproduce on any
accuracy metric. On HumanEval pass@1 the same matrix gives
$\rho = +1.00$ on Llama-3.1, so the GSM8K inversion does not
replicate, eliminating it as a cross-metric mechanism. The outlier
cell remains empirically unexplained on a single metric.

\paragraph{Per-seed stability.} Every entry in
Table~\ref{tab:downstream} is a mean over three independently trained
adapter seeds, exposing the merge's sensitivity to the adapter draw.
For the five matrix baselines the across-seed SD is $\lesssim 0.05$ on
every cell the authors examined ($\leq 0.025$ on all GSM8K cells), comfortably
below the method-to-method gaps that drive the Spearman rankings. The
exception is rd-encoder ridge on its two losing cells: SD $0.074$ on
Llama-3.1 GSM8K (per-seed $0.14$--$0.31$) and $0.084$ on Mistral
HumanEval ($0.08$--$0.28$), versus $\leq 0.015$ on the six cells it
wins or ties. This is the quantitative basis for the retraction in
\S\ref{subsec:exp-downstream-gsm8k}: earlier single-draw figures for
those two cells ($0.364$, $0.311$) sat near the top of a wide
across-seed range whose mean is much lower. The Llama-3.1 GSM8K
\emph{baseline} inversion is, by contrast, seed-robust: TIES
($0.256$, per-seed $0.24$--$0.27$) sits below KnOTS ($0.323$,
per-seed $0.32$--$0.34$) on every seed.

\paragraph{Statistical power.} Each per-cell $\rho$ is computed across
only the five matrix baselines ($n = 5$): exact permutation tests give
two-sided $p \approx 0.017$ for $\rho = 1.00$, $p \approx 0.08$ for
$\rho = 0.90$, and $p \approx 0.35$ for the Llama inversion
($\rho = -0.60$), so no single cell is individually conclusive. The
across-cell pattern ($7$ of $8$ positive; one-sided sign test
$p \approx 0.035$) carries the signal, with the caveat that the $8$
cells are not fully independent (shared bases, merges, and NLL
values), so the sign test is approximate and somewhat overstates
significance; the authors lean on it as corroboration, not a formal test.
