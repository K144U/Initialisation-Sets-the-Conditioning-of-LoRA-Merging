\label{subsec:exp-multiseed-bootstrap}
The headline ordering of the $T = 4$ matrix is verified against seed
variance by retraining the matrix adapters with three independent
seeds ($r = 16$, identical hyperparameters, seeds $\{1, 2, 3\}$) and
computing $10{,}000$-resample paired bootstrap $95\%$ CIs on the five
method-pair gaps that drive the recommendations of
\S\ref{sec:practical-recommendations}.

\paragraph{Decision rules.} For a gap $\Delta = \Phi^\star_A -
\Phi^\star_B$: \textbf{Confident} if the $95\%$ paired bootstrap CI
does not cross zero; \textbf{Borderline} if it crosses zero but
$|\Delta|$ exceeds half the CI half-width; \textbf{Inconclusive}
otherwise.

\paragraph{Result.} \emph{Every one of the $20$ method-pair gaps is
Confident} (Table~\ref{tab:multiseed-bootstrap}). The largest CI
half-width is $\pm 0.0044$ nats/token (Qwen TA$-$TVQ$_2$); the
tightest-margin gap is $|\Delta_{\mathrm{ties-tvq_2}}| = 0.0076$ on
Mistral-7B, whose CI $[-0.0099, -0.0043]$ still excludes zero.
