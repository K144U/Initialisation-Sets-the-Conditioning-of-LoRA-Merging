All theoretical claims are stated with explicit assumptions in
\S\ref{sec:setup} and proved in \S\ref{sec:lower}--\S\ref{sec:achv}
and the supplementary material. For the empirical results we release,
on publication, the training and evaluation code, exact per-task
configuration files (base model, LoRA rank and target modules,
optimizer, token budget, eval split sizes), random seeds, and the
per-example NLL arrays underlying every cell of the merge matrix.
Training and evaluation splits are disjoint (verified by a separate
zero-overlap audit); confidence intervals are $95\%$ percentile
bootstrap over $10{,}000$ resamples, with paired bootstraps for
method-to-method comparisons; point estimates were reproduced across
two independent evaluation runs to within $0.001$ nats/token. The
headline $T = 4$ matrix uses three independent training seeds
(Appendix~\ref{app:multiseed}); the $T$-scaling sweep and added
baselines use a single training seed, as stated in their sections.
The pre-registered Mistral prediction of
\S\ref{subsec:exp-T-scaling} is verifiable from the anonymized,
timestamped experiment-log bundle in the supplementary material,
whose commit trail shows no result commit predating the registration.
Appendix~\ref{app:manifest} gives a per-experiment manifest (cells,
reproducer script, per-cell wallclock); the total footprint of the
empirical results is roughly $80$--$120$ GPU-hours on a single
A100-80GB plus adapter training. A chronological log of design
decisions and intermediate negative results accompanies the
camera-ready version.
